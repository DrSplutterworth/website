About Dr. Cyril Splutterworth


How do you do, fellow gamers?
I'm an indie game developer from England, specialising in PC games.
I started out making learning games, with my Let's Learn Japanese! and Let's Learn Korean! series.
I have since moved into making retro-style arcade games.
I hope you find this website interesting. It serves as a portfolio for all the games I've published so far.
